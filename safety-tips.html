Personal Safety & Street Smarts

Cultivate active situational awareness and confident physical defense postures on Indian streets.

👁️

Situational Awareness

Keep your awareness active on crowded or deserted streets:

  • Scan ahead for unlit alleyways, construction chokepoints, or gathering crowds.
  • Limit smartphone distraction; keep eyes up and alert.
  • Keep at least one ear free from earbuds or headphones at all times.
  • Identify nearest open pharmacies, 24/7 ATM booths with guards, and petrol pumps.
🚶‍♀️

Assertive Body Language

Deter potential harassers through body posture:

  • Walk with purposeful strides, upright posture, and shoulders back.
  • Make brief, firm eye contact with oncoming passersby to signal active awareness.
  • Keep hands free from deep pockets so you can maneuver quickly.
  • Hold keys firmly in your hand before approaching apartment doors or gates.
⚡

Trust Your Instincts

Your gut intuition detects subtle anomalies immediately:

  • Never worry about appearing impolite or dramatic if a person makes you uncomfortable.
  • Cross the road immediately if someone matches your pace or follows behind.
  • Step into any populated shop or metro station gate if you feel pursued.
  • Firmly assert boundaries in local language or Hindi: "Peeche hatiye (Step back)."

Travel & Public Transport Safety

Protocols for cabs (Ola/Uber), autos, metros, local trains, and Indian Railways.

🚗

Ola / Uber Cab Verification

Always verify before stepping into any rideshare:

  • Match Registration Number: Check vehicle license plate carefully against app.
  • Ask "Who are you picking up?": Never volunteer your own name first.
  • Child Lock Check: Verify the back door handle opens smoothly from inside.
  • Track Route on Google Maps: Ensure the driver stays on the designated GPS route.
🚆

Metros & Indian Railways

Transit precautions for metro systems and long journeys:

  • Use designated Women-Only Coaches (Delhi Metro, Mumbai Locals, Namma Metro).
  • Save Indian Railways Security Helpline 139 (Rail Madad) on speed dial.
  • Wait under bright platform illumination near station master or RPF security booths.
  • Note the emergency intercom location inside coaches to speak with the train operator.
🛺

Autos & Night Commutes

Navigating late-night city commutes safely:

  • Photograph the auto-rickshaw registration number and driver badge if travelling late.
  • Share WhatsApp Live Location with a family member or trusted friend.
  • Make a clear phone call upon boarding: "I'm in auto DL 1R 2345, reaching in 15 mins."
  • Avoid taking shared autos with unknown male passengers late at night.

Campus & Workplace Safety (POSH Rights)

Institutional protections under the POSH Act 2013 and campus security procedures.

⚖️

Know Your POSH Rights

Protection of Women from Sexual Harassment Act 2013:

  • Every organization with 10+ employees must maintain an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC).
  • Harassment includes physical contact, sexually colored remarks, and non-verbal misconduct.
  • Complaints can be submitted within 3 months of an incident to the presiding officer.
  • The law guarantees protection against retaliation, job demotion, or victim harassment.
🏫

College & University Safety

Navigating after-hours labs, hostels, and libraries:

  • Save your campus chief security officer and women's development cell contacts.
  • Use campus escort or security accompany services when departing labs late at night.
  • Form peer walking groups with hostel friends when traversing campus pathways.
  • Report eve-teasing or stalking immediately to campus authorities and anti-ragging cells.
🏢

Late Night Office Shifts & Cabs

Safety protocols for IT/BPO and corporate late shifts:

  • Companies are legally mandated to provide security escorts for women dropping off late.
  • Ensure the company-provided cab drops you directly at your doorstep, not main roads.
  • Verify that the designated office security guard is present inside the vehicle.
  • Park personal vehicles in illuminated spots close to office security gates.

Online & Social Media Safety

Preserving digital sovereignty, preventing cyberstalking, and reporting cyber harassment in India.

🌐

Location Privacy & Social Feeds

Prevent strangers and stalkers from mapping your routines:

  • Never post real-time restaurant or venue check-ins; share stories after departing.
  • Disable geotagging metadata in your mobile camera settings.
  • Avoid posting pictures displaying vehicle number plates or apartment entrance boards.
  • Turn off Snapchat Snap Map and audit Instagram broadcast locations.
🔑

WhatsApp & Account Hardening

Defend against unauthorized access, hacking, and impersonation:

  • Enable WhatsApp Two-Step Verification with a custom 6-digit PIN.
  • Regularly audit "Linked Devices" in WhatsApp to ensure no unauthorized Web sessions.
  • Set WhatsApp Privacy so only contacts can see your "About", "Status", and Profile Photo.
  • Use app locks (fingerprint/Face ID) for sensitive messaging and gallery applications.
🛡️

Reporting Cyber Harassment (1930)

Immediate actions for online blackmail, morphed photos, or doxxing:

  • Capture unedited full-screen screenshots showing user handles, timestamps, and URLs.
  • Do not pay ransoms or delete message threads before filing an official complaint.
  • File an immediate complaint on the official portal cybercrime.gov.in or dial 1930.
  • Use StopNCII.org to cryptographically prevent non-consensual image sharing.

Emergency Preparedness & 112 Setup

Activate rapid distress triggers on your smartphone and prepare contingency safety plans.

📱

112 India App & Hardware SOS

Trigger silent emergency alerts without looking at your screen:

  • Install the official 112 India App (Ministry of Home Affairs ERSS).
  • Indian Mobile Mandate: Pressing the power button 3 times (feature phones: pressing 5 or 9) triggers emergency call to 112.
  • iPhone Emergency SOS: Click side button 5 times to dispatch coordinates to 112.
  • Enable "Medical ID" on your lock screen with blood group and ICE contacts.
👥

Trusted Contact Circle

Coordinate with 2-3 reliable people in your local network:

  • Designate one primary contact who picks up phone calls 24/7.
  • Create a secret duress phrase (e.g., "Aunty is asking for that Hindi recipe") meaning Call 112 to my location.
  • Ensure they possess your workplace address, daily route, and PG/hostel details.
  • Establish an agreement to call if you miss a scheduled check-in by 20 minutes.
🎒

Discreet Emergency Kit

If you ever need to exit an unsafe environment urgently:

  • Aadhaar card, PAN card, voter ID, driving license, and bank passbooks (or scans).
  • Emergency cash notes tucked securely in a discreet compartment.
  • Fully charged power bank, charging cable, and spare house/vehicle keys.
  • Personal pull-pin high decibel safety alarm for immediate crowd attention.
