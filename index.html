Three essential security habits you can incorporate into your daily routine right now.

Critical Habit

Cab & Auto Verification Rule

Never enter a cab, taxi, or auto-rickshaw until you verify these checkpoints:

  • Match Plate & OTP: Confirm vehicle registration plate with the Ola/Uber app.
  • Ask "Who are you picking up?": Ensure the driver states your name first.
  • Live Journey Tracking: Share WhatsApp Live Location or in-app trip status with family.

Digital Security

Set Up Emergency SOS on 112

Equip your phone to reach ERSS 112 instantly without unlocking:

  • Android Panic Button: Press power button 3 or 5 times to dispatch coordinates.
  • iPhone Emergency SOS: Rapidly click side button 5 times to trigger ERSS 112.
  • 112 India Official App: Install the government ERSS app to transmit silent panic alerts.

Street Smarts

Street Awareness & Intuition

How you navigate public spaces deters potential harassment:

  • Heads Up, Ears Open: Keep headphones at low volume and scan approaching routes.
  • Safe Commercial Havens: Step inside well-lit shops, banks, or metro stations if followed.
  • Know "Zero FIR" Rights: Any police station must register an FIR irrespective of location.

Key Awareness Pillars

  • Clear Boundaries: You have the legal and personal right to say "No".
  • Zero FIR Rights: File complaints at any police station across India.
  • POSH Protections: Mandatory anti-harassment committees in workplaces.
