Are You in Immediate Physical Danger?

If someone is actively threatening you, following you, or attempting to harm you, immediately reach a populated area or shop and dial the Pan-India emergency response line.

Emergency Response Support System (ERSS 112)

The Government of India's single unified emergency number connecting citizens to Police, Fire, and Ambulance teams across all States and Union Territories.

Pan-India ERSS All 28 States & 8 UTs

112 National Emergency

112

Single emergency number for all crises in India (integrating Police 100, Fire 101, and Ambulance 108). Can be dialed from any mobile or landline without a SIM card or mobile balance.

Call 112 Now

SMS to 112

If speaking on a call puts you in grave danger, send an SMS directly to 112 with your location and nature of emergency. The ERSS state control room will dispatch a patrol vehicle to your coordinates.

Police & Ambulance Services in India

Direct telephone lines for rapid police intervention, emergency medical transport, and trauma assistance.

Law Enforcement Pan-India Direct Police

Police Control Room

100 / 112

Contact immediately for physical threats, assault, stalking, eve-teasing, or ongoing danger.

Your Legal Rights with Indian Police:
  • Zero FIR Right: Any police station MUST register your FIR regardless of jurisdiction. It is later transferred to the concerned police station.
  • Female Officer Presence: Statements by female victims of sexual offences must be recorded by a woman police officer.
  • No Night Arrest: Women cannot be arrested after sunset and before sunrise except in extraordinary circumstances with a magistrate's order.
Call Police (100)
Emergency Health All Indian States

Ambulance & Healthcare Response

108 (Emergency) | 102 (Maternity/Child)

Contact for severe physical trauma, medical distress, poisoning, or immediate hospital dispatch.

Medical Support & Forensic Care:
  • Sakhi One Stop Centres (OSC): Located at district government hospitals, providing integrated free medical care, police assistance, and psycho-social counseling under one roof.
  • Preserving Forensic Evidence: In cases of sexual assault, avoid washing, bathing, or changing clothes before an official medico-legal examination.
Call Ambulance (108)

Dedicated section for "Report Crime Against Women & Children". You can file complaints anonymously without revealing personal details, or register a formal FIR for police investigation.

Open Cyber Crime Portal ↗

Checklist Before Filing a Cyber Complaint:

  • Uncropped Screenshots: Capture the complete screen showing URL address, account handle, date, and device time.
  • Profile Links: Copy the exact profile link (URL) of the harasser before they delete or change their handle.
  • Preserve WhatsApp Chats: Use "Export Chat Without Media" to obtain a raw timestamped text log with phone numbers.
  • Never Pay Extortionists: Paying ransoms leads to repeated demands. Report directly to 1930.

Establishing a Trusted Contacts Network in India

How to synchronize with family, friends, and the 112 ERSS system.

1. Pre-Agree on Local Code Words

Create an innocent phrase that secretly means "Call 112 to my location immediately." Example: "Did Mummy get that courier package from Delhi?"

2. Configure 112 India App

Add up to 3 trusted contacts inside the official 112 India App. When you trigger the panic button, they simultaneously receive SMS alerts with your real-time GPS link.

3. Enable Medical ID / ICE Contacts

Add parents or local guardians under your smartphone's "In Case of Emergency" (ICE) contacts, so doctors or first responders can view them without unlocking your phone.
